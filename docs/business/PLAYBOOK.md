# Malik AI Business System — Operating Playbook

## Product

Malik AI Business sells one outcome-oriented system rather than disconnected freelance tasks:

**Premium website + AI consultant + Telegram/WhatsApp bot + lead capture + CRM flow + follow-up.**

Primary promise: reduce lost enquiries and make the path from visitor to qualified lead faster and clearer.

## Ideal customer profile

Start with service businesses where one additional customer can justify the system cost:

1. Dental clinics and private clinics
2. Beauty salons / aesthetic clinics
3. Training centres and language schools
4. Auto services / detailing
5. Real-estate agencies and developers

Prioritise businesses with one or more of these signals:
- outdated or slow website;
- no visible online booking;
- only a WhatsApp phone number with no qualification flow;
- many repetitive customer questions;
- multiple services but weak navigation;
- active social pages but weak owned website;
- ads running to a generic page;
- poor mobile UX;
- no 24/7 first response;
- no structured follow-up.

## Sales workflow

1. Find a target business.
2. Inspect website, booking flow, social links and obvious friction.
3. Record one specific problem — not generic criticism.
4. Send a short personalised outreach email/DM.
5. Offer a free mini-prototype, not a free full project.
6. If interested, collect niche, services, brand assets, current site and main conversion goal.
7. Build a 1-screen prototype + sample AI conversation + bot flow.
8. Present expected workflow, not guaranteed revenue.
9. Qualify budget and decision-maker.
10. Send scoped proposal.
11. Agree deliverables, timeline, ownership and payment terms.
12. Start after required payment/approval.
13. Launch staging.
14. Run QA on mobile/desktop and lead flow.
15. Obtain written approval.
16. Deploy production.
17. Offer monthly maintenance/optimisation.

## Qualification score

Score each lead 0-2 per dimension:
- Need: visible conversion/automation gap
- Value: customer economics can justify project
- Access: public business contact / decision-maker reachable
- Urgency: active ads, expansion, hiring, launch, weak current site
- Fit: site + AI + bot can materially improve workflow

8-10 = Hot
5-7 = High
3-4 = Normal
0-2 = Low

## Guardrails

- Never claim guaranteed revenue or guaranteed conversion lift.
- Never impersonate a client or publish from their account without explicit authorisation.
- For healthcare, AI provides general service/booking information only; no diagnosis or prescribing.
- For legal/financial/high-stakes areas, use appropriate professional review.
- Store only data required for the business relationship.
- Respect opt-outs and do not continue unsolicited follow-ups after a clear refusal.
- Avoid deceptive scarcity, fake case studies, fake reviews or fabricated metrics.

## Monthly recurring revenue

Offer post-launch care as a separate recurring plan:
- hosting / monitoring;
- small content changes;
- bot knowledge updates;
- AI prompt and knowledge optimisation;
- funnel analytics;
- monthly conversion review;
- integration maintenance.

Suggested starting range: 50,000-150,000 KZT/month depending on scope and usage.
