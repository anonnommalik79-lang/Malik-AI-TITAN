# Malik AI Business — Content Engine

## Core message

We do not sell “a website”. We build one customer-acquisition workflow:

**Website → AI consultant → Telegram/WhatsApp bot → qualified lead → human follow-up.**

Use proof, demos and before/after workflows. Avoid fake revenue screenshots, fake clients and guaranteed results.

## Content pillars

1. Website conversion problems
2. AI customer-service demos
3. Bot automation demos
4. Lead OS / CRM workflow
5. Build-in-public / founder engineering
6. Real client case studies after written permission

## Capture checklist for every demo video

- hide API keys, tokens and email inbox contents;
- use synthetic demo customer information unless permission exists;
- do not claim actual conversion/revenue improvements without data;
- keep UI text readable on mobile;
- show one clear customer journey per video;
- end with one CTA only.
