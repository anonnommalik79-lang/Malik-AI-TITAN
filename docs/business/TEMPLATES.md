## 5. Maintenance plan description

Monthly Malik AI Business Care may include, according to the selected tier:
- monitoring and integration maintenance;
- limited content changes;
- AI knowledge-base updates;
- bot copy updates;
- funnel/lead-flow review;
- monthly summary and recommendations.

Third-party usage fees and major redesign/development are separate unless explicitly included.

10. Next step

## 7. Invoice/payment milestone suggestion

For small custom projects, a simple commercial structure can be:
- 50% to start;
- 30% after staging demonstration;
- 20% before production handover/launch.

Adjust to project risk, local law, tax/accounting requirements and client negotiations. Do not start material paid work without a clear written agreement on payment and scope.
