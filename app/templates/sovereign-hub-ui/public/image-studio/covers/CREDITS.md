# Image studio covers

Every picture in this folder was generated by an open image model and taken
from the model's public showcase, published by its authors under the Apache
License 2.0. They were cropped to 4:3 and resized to 800×600. Malik AI replaces
each one with a cover painted by its own premium model (MalikImage 1.0 Premium,
FLUX.2 Dev) the first time the owner account opens the image studio.
